✦ About the Maestro

A Life Devoted to
Rhythmic Mastery

"Music is not merely performance; it is a lifelong discipline, a medium of cultural dialogue, and a pathway to personal transformation."

Born and raised in the culturally rich city of Lucknow, Dr. Shrikant Shukla discovered his passion for percussion at the age of seven. His musical journey began under the traditional Guru-Shishya Parampara, and he received rigorous gharana training from Khalifa Ustad Ilmas Hussain Khan, a distinguished master of the Lucknow Tabla tradition.

Dr. Shukla is an accomplished Tabla artist specializing in Tabla, Dholak, and Naal. He holds M.Mus., NET, and a Ph.D. in Tabla from Bhatkhande Music Institute Deemed University, completed under the guidance of Pt. Prof. Mukund Narayan Bhale.

He is a member of the Uttar Pradesh Sangeet Natak Akademi and recipient of the UP Sangeet Natak Akademi Samman. A B-High Grade Artist of All India Radio and former member of the National Kathak Sansthan, Lucknow (now Birju Maharaj Sansthan), he is also a National Fellowship awardee from CCRT, Ministry of Culture, Government of India.

Dr. Shukla has authored the book “Khalifa Ustad Afaq Hussain Khan and His Personality.” He has performed at prestigious national events such as Maihar Mahotsav, Taj Mahotsav, Kumbh, Ramotsav, Defence Expo, G-20 events, UP Summit, and other renowned cultural platforms.

He has served as Guest Faculty (Music) at Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar Central University, Lucknow, and continues to evaluate emerging talent as an examiner for Bhatkhande Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya, Indira Kala Sangeet Vishwavidyalaya, and numerous other government and private universities across India.

Saraswati Sangeet Academy

Transforming Music Education for Over Two Decades — Where Tradition Meets Excellence

✦ ESTABLISHED 2002, LUCKNOW

Founded by Dr. Shrikant Shukla in recognition of the need for structured and sustainable music education, Saraswati Sangeet Academy has trained hundreds of students across both offline and online platforms over more than two decades.

The academy offers a unique blend of traditional Indian classical instruction and internationally recognised certifications, making it a premier destination for serious music students.

Guided by a philosophy of inclusive and accessible music education, the academy also supports social initiatives aimed at providing music education opportunities to underprivileged students.
